The paraphrasing tool market hit $1.0–1.7B in 2025, driven by exploding content demand (digital content market projected at $400B+ by 2026) and e-learning growth ($375B by 2026). Academic writing is the dominant use case.

### Existential vs Manageable Risks

**Existential** (requires strategic pivot):
- AI text becomes fundamentally undetectable → Pivot to writing quality tools
- Google/Microsoft bundle free AI writing tools into Docs/Word → Specialize deeper in academic niche

**Manageable** (compete through execution):
- GPTZero/QuillBot enter South Asian market → First-mover, lower costs, STEM features
- Turnitin offers regional pricing → Individual access + better STEM support
- Free tools improve → Integrated workflow is the moat
