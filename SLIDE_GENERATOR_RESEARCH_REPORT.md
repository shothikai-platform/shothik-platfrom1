# AI Slide Generators - Market Research & Gap Analysis

## Date: February 24, 2026
## Research Scope: Qwen AI Slides, Gamma, Tome, Presentations.AI, Skywork

---

## 🎯 Current Market Leaders - Feature Analysis

### 1. Qwen AI Slides (Alibaba - Just Launched)

**Key Features:**
- ✅ **Qwen3 Agent** - Research agent that organizes story structure
- ✅ **Qwen-Image 2.0** - Generates complete slide visuals in one shot
- ✅ **Multi-input support** - Text, documents, or simple ideas
- ✅ **Visual-first approach** - Text, layout, colors, graphics all generated together
- ✅ **Search integration** - Auto-researches and builds narrative

**Innovation:**
- Not just text → slides, but **idea → visual story**
- Image generation directly integrated (not just templates)
- Agent-based research before generation

---

### 2. Gamma (Market Leader)

**Key Features:**
- ✅ **Card-based format** - Web-native, scrollable presentations
- ✅ **AI generation** - From prompts or documents
- ✅ **Real-time collaboration** - Comments, permissions, team workspaces
- ✅ **Interactive embeds** - Videos, charts, links
- ✅ **Multiple export formats** - PDF, PPTX, Google Slides
- ✅ **Viewer analytics** - Track who viewed what
- ✅ **Thematic layouts** - Visual storytelling optimized

**Pricing:**
- Free tier with credits
- Pro: $8/month
- Team plans available

**Strengths:**
- Best-in-class collaboration
- Web-first design (not slide-first)
- Strong community/templates

---

### 3. Tome

**Key Features:**
- ✅ **Storytelling focus** - Narrative-driven presentations
- ✅ **Interactive slides** - Animations, dynamic layouts
- ✅ **AI generation** - From prompts
- ✅ **Web-based sharing** - Instant link distribution
- ✅ **Figma integration**
- ✅ **Custom fonts** (Pro)

**Weaknesses:**
- Limited PPTX export quality
- Watermarks on free tier
- Not ideal for traditional slide decks

---

### 4. Presentations.AI

**Key Features:**
- ✅ **Conversational generation** - Chat-based slide creation
- ✅ **Brand Sync** - Consistent fonts, colors, themes
- ✅ **Rapid draft creation** - Fast first version
- ✅ **Multiple content types** - Infographics, charts

**Weaknesses:**
- Limited editing after generation
- No direct PPTX export
- Minimal collaboration features

---

### 5. Skywork

**Key Features:**
- ✅ **Best PPTX export** - Clean, editable PowerPoint files
- ✅ **Minimal branding** - Professional free tier
- ✅ **Reliable formatting** - Charts, layouts preserved
- ✅ **PDF export** - Clean, no watermarks

**Strengths:**
- Best for corporate/professional use
- PowerPoint ecosystem compatibility

## 🔍 Gaps in Shothik's Current Implementation

### Critical Gaps (High Priority)

#### 1. **Research Agent Integration** ❌ MISSING
**What competitors have:**
- Qwen: Qwen3 Agent researches and builds story structure
- Gamma: Can import and structure content

**Shothik gap:**
- No research phase before slide generation
- No content structuring from documents
- No web search integration

**Impact:** Users must manually structure content

---

#### 2. **Visual/Image Generation** ❌ MISSING
**What competitors have:**
- Qwen: Qwen-Image 2.0 generates complete visuals
- Gamma: Thematic visual canvases
- Others: Template-based design

**Shothik gap:**
- Only template-based slides
- No AI-generated images/layouts
- No visual storyboarding

**Impact:** Slides look generic, not custom-designed

---

#### 3. **Document Import & Parsing** ❌ MISSING
**What competitors have:**
- Qwen: Upload document → auto-generates slides
- Gamma: Import from Notion/docs
- Skywork: Document-to-presentation

**Shothik gap:**
- No document upload feature
- No PDF/Word import
- No URL scraping for content

**Impact:** Users must manually copy-paste content

---

#### 4. **Undo/Redo System** ❌ MISSING
**What competitors have:**
- All major tools have full undo/redo

**Shothik gap:**
- Partial implementation (75% complete)

- No history visualization

**Impact:** Users afraid to experiment, can't recover mistakes
